GoPro · Product Evaluation Tester · Jan to Mar 2022, Maui, HI

Field testing what became the GoPro Volta

A short early gig, and my first taste of structured product development: field testing a functional pre-release accessory prototype for GoPro and feeding usability findings back to the team before launch.

GoPro placed a working prototype of an unreleased accessory with testers on Maui, and I put it through real use: surf, sand, salt water, and everyday handling, the environment the product was actually built for. My job was to report structured usability feedback, what worked, what fought me, what broke and how, in a form the development team could act on. The product shipped as the GoPro Volta.

It was a small role, but it is where I first saw the loop I now work inside from the other end: prototype, put it in real conditions, write down what actually happened, change the design.
